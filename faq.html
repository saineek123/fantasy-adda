FAQ

💰 Recharge kaise kare?

Recharge page pe amount select karo, UPI se pay karo, UTR daalo aur submit karo. Admin 5 min - 2 hours me approve karega.

🏦 Withdrawal kab milega?

Withdrawal request admin approval ke baad 24-48 hours me process hoti hai. Bank me aane me 1-3 working days lag sakte hain.

💸 15% commission kyun?

Platform maintenance aur services ke liye net winnings par 15% commission charge hota hai.

🎁 Referral bonus kab milega?

Jab aapka friend ₹100+ ka first recharge kare, tab aapko ₹100 bonus 24 hours me milega.

🔒 Account safe hai?

Haan, aapka account Firebase se secured hai. Apna password kisi se share na karein.

📞 Support se kaise baat kare?

Profile → Help & Support me jaake ticket submit karo ya WhatsApp par message karo.
